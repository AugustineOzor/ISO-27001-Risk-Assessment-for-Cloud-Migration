# 📐 Scoring Legend

> Reference scales used throughout this assessment for risk scoring and control effectiveness.

## Likelihood & Impact Scale

| Score | Likelihood | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Negligible |
| 2 | Unlikely | Minor |
| 3 | Possible | Moderate |
| 4 | Likely | Major |
| 5 | Almost Certain | Severe |

## Risk Score Bands

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

| Score Range | Band |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | ![Low](https://img.shields.io/badge/risk-Low-28a745) |
| 5–9 | ![Medium](https://img.shields.io/badge/risk-Medium-dbab09) |
| 10–14 | ![High](https://img.shields.io/badge/risk-High-e36209) |
| 15–25 | ![Critical](https://img.shields.io/badge/risk-Critical-b60205) |

## Control Effectiveness Scale

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ![Effective](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Effective-28a745) | Control consistently prevents/detects the risk as designed |
| ![Partially Effective](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Partially%20Effective-dbab09) | Control reduces risk but has known gaps |
| ![Ineffective](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Ineffective-d73a49) | Control does not reliably prevent/detect the risk |
| ![Not Tested](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Not%20Tested-6a737d) | Control exists but has not been validated |
